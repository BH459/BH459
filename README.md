### 🚀 InternHub

**Stack:** `MERN` · `FastAPI` · `Python`

Dual-module intelligent system for Internship Scheme Portal, combining personalized internship recommendations with smart candidate allocation.

<details>
<summary><strong>View Features</strong></summary>

<br>

### 🤖 Smart Recruitment Assistant

**Stack:** `Gemini API` · `Next.js` · `Node.js` · `MongoDB`

AI-powered recruitment assistant that analyzes resumes against job descriptions using semantic AI matching, ATS scoring, skill-gap detection, and interview evaluation.

<details>
<summary><strong>View Features</strong></summary>

<br>

**→ Semantic Resume Matching**  
Uses Gemini API to compare resumes with job descriptions, measure alignment, and identify missing technical or soft skills for the target role.

**→ ATS Score & Optimization**  
Generates an ATS score from 0–100 based on resume keywords, structure, formatting, sections, and role-specific keyword relevance.

**→ AI Resume Suggestions**  
Provides actionable recommendations to improve experience descriptions, achievements, keywords, and overall resume impact.

**→ Formatting Validation**  
Checks resume layout, fonts, sections, and ATS-friendly structure to improve visibility in applicant tracking systems.

**→ Resume History Tracking**  
Stores multiple resume versions and tracks score improvements over time to monitor candidate growth.

**→ AI Interview Evaluation**  
Evaluates interview transcripts with JD-aligned scoring, performance insights, and smart follow-up question generation.

</details>

Full-stack event management platform with role-based portals for Admins, Volunteers, and Students, enabling event registration, payment collection, QR ticketing, and real-time revenue tracking.

<details>
<summary><strong>View Features</strong></summary>

<br>

**→ Admin Portal**  
Super Admin can assign admin credentials, manage events, assign volunteer roles, track real-time revenue dashboards, filter sales reports, and export student registration lists to Excel.

**→ Volunteer Portal**  
Volunteers can view participant lists, process cash payments, generate receipts, track personal collection totals, and redeem tickets using an integrated scanner.

**→ Student Portal**  
Students can self-register, browse available events, register for events, track approval status, view registration history, and access unique QR tickets for entry and food coupons.

**→ QR Ticket Scanner**  
Fast QR-based verification system for gate entry and food coupon redemption, helping reduce queues and ensure smooth event flow.

### 📦 Sales & Inventory Management

**Stack:** `Next.js` · `Node.js` · `MongoDB` · `Real-Time Analytics`

Multi-tenant sales and inventory management platform that helps organizations manage products, track employee sales, monitor inventory, and analyze revenue performance in real time.

<details>
<summary><strong>View Features</strong></summary>

<br>

**→ Admin Portal**  
Admins can create unique organization codes, manage products, update MRP and selling prices, track employee sales activities, and monitor real-time dashboard analytics.

**→ Product & Inventory Management**  
Supports adding, editing, and deleting products with real-time inventory updates to keep stock and pricing data accurate across the organization.

**→ Employee Portal**  
Employees can join an organization using a unique code, view available products, process sales, and track their personal sales history.

**→ QR-Based Sales Flow**  
Integrated camera-based QR scanner automatically adds scanned products to the cart, improving checkout speed and reducing manual entry.

**→ Sales Reports & Analytics**  
Provides monthly sales reports with charts, filters by month, year, and organization, and real-time insights into revenue trends.

</details>
